The process begins with the "Claim Registration". After the claim is registered, it goes through an "Examination by Claims Officer". Following the examination, a "Settlement Recommendation" is written. This recommendation is then checked in the "Check Recommendation" step. 

After the recommendation has been checked, the process reaches a decision point - "Claim OK or Not OK". If the claim is not OK, it goes back to the "Examination by Claims Officer" for further scrutiny. 

If the claim is OK, it moves to the "Handle Claim" step. After the claim has been handled, the process ends.
