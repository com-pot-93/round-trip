The process begins at the Evanstonian, specifically in the Room Service lane. The first action is to take an order. Once the order is taken, an order ticket is submitted to the kitchen. After the kitchen receives the order ticket, the order is given to the sommelier. The sommelier then assigns the order to a waiter. The waiter is responsible for preparing the cart. Once the cart is prepared, the waiter delivers the order. After the order is delivered, the guest's account is debited. The process ends after the guest's account is debited.
