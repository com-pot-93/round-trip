The process begins with "MPON sends the dismissal". After this, "MPOO reviews the dismissal". Following the review, a decision is made to either oppose or confirm the dismissal. If the decision is to oppose, "MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON" takes place, leading to the end of the process. If the decision is to confirm, "MPOO confirms the dismissal of MPON" takes place, also leading to the end of the process. During the process, a message is sent from "MPON sends the dismissal" to "MPOO reviews the dismissal". The process involves two main participants, MPON and MPOO, each with their own respective lanes of activities.
