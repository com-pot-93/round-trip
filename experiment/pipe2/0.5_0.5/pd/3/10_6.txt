The process begins with "MSPN sends a dismissal to the MSPO". After this, "MSPO reviews the dismissal". Following the review, a decision is made at the "MSPO Decision" point. If the decision is to reject the dismissal, the process follows the path "MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN" and then ends. Alternatively, if the decision is to confirm the dismissal, the process follows the path "MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN" and then also ends.
