The process begins with "Examine claim". After the claim is examined, "Write settlement recommendation" is the next step. Once the recommendation is written, it is then checked in the "Check recommendation" step. The process then arrives at "Claim OK?" decision point. If the claim is OK, it proceeds to "Handle claim". If the claim is not OK, it loops back to the beginning and the claim is re-examined. The process ends after the claim has been handled.
