The process begins with MPON sending the dismissal to the MPOO. Once the dismissal has been sent, the MPOO reviews it. After the review, MPOO makes a decision. If the MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON, the process ends. If the MPOO confirms the dismissal of the MPON, the process also ends. The actions of MPON and MPOO are carried out in their respective lanes, with MPON only performing the action of sending the dismissal and MPOO performing the review, making a decision, and either opposing or confirming the dismissal. The sequence of actions is directed by various flows. There is also a message flow from the action of MPON sending the dismissal to the action of MPOO reviewing the dismissal.
