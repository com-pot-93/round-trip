The process begins with the "Start" event, from which the "Submit vacation request" activity is initiated. Once the vacation request is submitted, it goes to the "Supervise request" activity. 

After the supervision of the request, a decision is made at the "Supervisor decision" point. Depending on the decision made, the process either goes to the "Review rejection reasons" activity or the "Complete management procedures" activity. 

If the process goes to the "Review rejection reasons" activity, it means the vacation request was declined, and reasons for the rejection are reviewed. After reviewing the rejection reasons, the process ends. 

If the process goes to the "Complete management procedures" activity, it means the vacation request was approved, and management procedures are completed. After the completion of management procedures, the process ends. 

The process involves three roles: the Employee, who submits the vacation request and reviews rejection reasons if any; the Supervisor, who supervises the request and makes the decision; and Human Resources, who complete the management procedures if the vacation request is approved.
