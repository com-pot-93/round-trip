The process begins with "INQ" transmitting a transaction data request. After this, "IP" checks the request. Depending on the outcome of this check, which is determined at the "Check request outcome" stage, "IP" then answers the question. The process concludes with an end event. The process flows from "INQ" to "IP", with message flows between the tasks of transmitting the transaction data request, checking the request, and answering the question.
