The process begins with "INQ transmits transaction data request". After this, the "IP checks the request". Depending on the outcome of the "Check request outcome", the "IP answers the question". The process ends after this step. The process takes place in two main sections or pools, namely "INQ" and "IP". In the "INQ" pool, the tasks "INQ transmits transaction data request" and "IP checks the request" are executed. In the "IP" pool, the task "IP answers the question" is performed. The transition from one task to another is represented by sequence flows and message flows. The sequence flows are from "start" to "INQ transmits transaction data request", from "INQ transmits transaction data request" to "IP checks the request", from "IP checks the request" to "Check request outcome", from "Check request outcome" to "IP answers the question", and from "IP answers the question" to "end". The message flows are from "INQ transmits transaction data request" to "IP checks the request" and from "IP checks the request" to "IP answers the question".
