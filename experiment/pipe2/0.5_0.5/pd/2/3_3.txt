The process begins with the "Claim Examination". After this, it moves to "Write Settlement Recommendation". Following this, the process proceeds to "Check Recommendation". After the recommendation has been checked, it goes to "Claim OK or Not OK". From here, the process can take two paths. One path goes back to "Write Settlement Recommendation" and the other path continues to "Handle Claim". If the process continues to "Handle Claim", it then ends.
