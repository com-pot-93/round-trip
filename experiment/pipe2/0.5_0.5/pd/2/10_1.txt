The process starts with "MPON sends the dismissal to MPOO". After this, "MPOO reviews the dismissal". Following the review, there is a decision point named "MPOO decision". Depending on the decision, two paths can be taken. If MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON, the process takes a path where "MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON" and the process ends. If MPOO confirms the dismissal of MPON, the process takes a different path where "MPOO confirms the dismissal of MPON" and then the process ends. The process is divided into two pools, one for MPON and the other for MPOO. The MPON pool includes the task of sending the dismissal, while the MPOO pool includes the tasks of reviewing, opposing or confirming the dismissal, and the decision point.
