The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. After the dismissal has been sent, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, a decision on the dismissal is made. This decision can lead to two outcomes: either the MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN or the MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN. Both outcomes mark the end of the process. The sequence of these actions is facilitated by various flows that dictate the order of tasks. There is also a message flow from the MSPN's task of sending the dismissal to the MSPO's task of reviewing it.
