The process begins at 'start' and proceeds to 'Take down the order'. After taking down the order, the next step is to 'Submit order ticket to the kitchen'. Once the order ticket is submitted to the kitchen, the order is then given to the sommelier through the 'Give order to the sommelier' step. After the sommelier receives the order, it's then assigned to the waiter via 'Assign order to the waiter'. The waiter then readies a cart in the 'Waiter readies a cart' step. Once the cart is ready, the order is delivered to the guest room through the 'Deliver order to the guest room' step. After the order has been delivered, the guest's account is debited in the 'Debit the guest's account' step. The process then ends at 'end'. This process is part of the 'Room Service' lane in 'The Evanstonian' pool.
