The process begins with the "start" event. The first task is "MSPN sents a dismissal to the MSPO". After this, the "MSPO reviews the dismissal". Once the review is done, the process reaches the "MSPO Decision" stage. Here, two outcomes are possible. The "MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN" or the "MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN". Both outcomes lead to the "end" event. The message flow indicates that the dismissal from the "MSPN" is sent to the "MSPO" for review. The process involves two pools, namely "MSPN" and "MSPO".
