The process begins with "Check defect and calculate repair cost". Once this is completed, it proceeds to "Is cost acceptable?". Based on the outcome of this decision, the process moves to "Customer decision". After the customer makes a decision, the process can either go to "Check and repair hardware" or "Check and configure software". Both these paths converge at "Test system functionality". After testing the system functionality, the process moves to "Is system functioning properly?". Depending on the result, the process ends.
