The process begins with the action "MPON sends dismissal". This action is then followed by "MPOO reviews dismissal". Following the review, there is a decision point, "Decision to oppose or confirm dismissal". Depending on the decision made, one of two actions are taken. If the dismissal is opposed, the action "MPOO opposes dismissal" is taken, otherwise, the action "MPOO confirms dismissal" is taken. Both of these actions lead to the end of the process. The actions taken by MPON and MPOO are separated into two different lanes, "MPON actions" and "MPOO actions", respectively. There is also a message flow from "MPON sends dismissal" to "MPOO reviews dismissal".
