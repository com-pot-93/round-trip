The process begins with "INQ transmits the transaction data request to the IP". Following which, "IP checks the request of the INQ". After checking the request, there is an "Outcome of the examination". Based on the outcome, "IP answers the question of the INQ". The process ends after the IP answers the question. This process involves two main entities, INQ and IP. INQ is responsible for transmitting the transaction data request to IP and IP checks the request, examines the outcome and answers the question of INQ.
