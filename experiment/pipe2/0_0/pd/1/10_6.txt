The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. Once the dismissal has been sent, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, the MSPO makes a decision. This decision can be to either reject the dismissal of the MSPN or confirm the dismissal of the MSPN. The process ends after the MSPO has made their decision. The tasks of sending a dismissal and making a decision are performed by the MSPN and MSPO respectively.
