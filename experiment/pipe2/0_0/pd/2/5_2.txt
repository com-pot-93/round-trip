The process begins with the "Submit Vacation Request" action. Once this is completed, it moves on to the "Supervisor Approval" action. Following this, a decision is made at the "Supervisor Decision" point. Depending on the outcome of this decision, the process may either move to the "Review Rejection Reasons" action or the "HR Management Procedures" action. If the "Review Rejection Reasons" action is taken, the process returns to the "Supervisor Approval" action. On the other hand, if the "HR Management Procedures" action is taken, the process comes to an end. The actions are divided among three roles: the Employee, who is responsible for submitting the vacation request and reviewing rejection reasons; the Supervisor, who is responsible for approving the request and making a decision; and HR, who manages the procedures.
