The process begins at "start" in the "Room Service" lane of "The Evanstonian" pool. The first action taken is to "Take down the order". After the order is taken, it is then "Submitted to the kitchen". Once the kitchen has received the order, it is then given to the sommelier. After the sommelier has the order, it is "Assigned to a waiter". The waiter then "Readies a cart" for the order. Once the cart is ready, the waiter "Delivers the order". After the order has been delivered, the waiter "Debits the guest's account". The process ends at "end".
