The process begins with "MPON sends the dismissal to the MPOO". Then, "MPOO reviews the dismissal". After reviewing, "MPOO" needs to make a decision at the point named "Oppose or confirm decision". If the decision is to oppose, the process continues with "MPOO opposes the dismissal" and then it ends. If the decision is to confirm, the process continues with "MPOO confirms the dismissal" and then it ends. The tasks are divided into two pools: one for "MPON" and another for "MPOO". The "MPON" pool contains the task of sending the dismissal and the start of the process. The "MPOO" pool contains the tasks of reviewing, opposing or confirming the dismissal, the decision point, and the end of the process. The tasks are connected with sequence flows and there is a message flow from "MPON sends the dismissal to the MPOO" to "MPOO reviews the dismissal".
