The process takes place at The Evanstonian Hotel, specifically within the Room Service department. The process begins with taking down the order. Once the order has been taken, it is then submitted to the kitchen. After submission, the order is given to the sommelier. The sommelier then assigns the order to the waiter. Once the waiter has the order, they prepare the cart and gather the silverware. The order is then delivered to the guest room. Finally, the guest account is debited. The process ends after the guest account is debited.
