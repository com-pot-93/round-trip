The process begins with "Check Defect and Calculate Repair Cost". After this step, a decision is made at the "Customer Decision" point. Depending on the result of this decision, the process moves to either "Check and Repair Hardware" or "Check and Configure Software". Both of these paths converge at "Test System Functionality". After the system functionality is tested, another decision is made at the "Error Detection" point. Depending on the result of this decision, the process either ends or returns to "Check and Repair Hardware" or "Check and Configure Software". This entire process belongs to the "Repair Process" lane within the "CRS" pool.
