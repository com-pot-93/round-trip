The process starts with the "Submit Vacation Request". After this, it moves to the "Review Request". Once the request has been reviewed, it goes to the "Approve/Reject Request". Depending on the outcome of the approval or rejection, the process may go to either "Review Rejection Reasons" or "Complete Management Procedures". If the request was rejected, it will be reviewed for rejection reasons. If approved, the management procedures will be completed. Both these paths lead to the end of the process.

This process involves different roles within the organization. The "Employee" is responsible for submitting the vacation request and reviewing any rejection reasons. The "Supervisor" reviews and approves or rejects the request. If the request is approved, the "HR Representative" completes the management procedures.
