The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. Once the MSPN has sent the dismissal, it is then reviewed by the MSPO. Following the review, the MSPO must make a decision on the dismissal. This decision can result in one of two outcomes. The MSPO can either reject the dismissal of the MSPN or confirm the dismissal of the MSPN. Regardless of the decision made, the process concludes once the MSPO has made their decision.
