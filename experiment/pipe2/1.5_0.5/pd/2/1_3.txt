The process begins at the "Room Service at The Evanstonian" where the "Room Service Manager" takes an order from a guest. Once the order is taken, it is then submitted to the kitchen. The Room Service Manager may also order wine from a sommelier if required. After the order is prepared, the "Waiter" is responsible for preparing the cart. The waiter then delivers the order to the guest. Once the order is delivered, the guest's account is debited. This marks the end of the process. Throughout the process, the tasks flow in a sequence from taking the order to debiting the guest's account.
