The process begins with the event 'start'. Following this, 'MPON sends the dismissal to the MPOO'. Afterwards, 'MPOO reviews the dismissal'. The next step depends on 'MPOO Decision'. If the decision is to oppose, then 'MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON' takes place. If the decision is to confirm, then 'MPOO confirms the dismissal of MPON' takes place. Both of these paths lead to the 'end' event. The process involves two groups of activities, one set happening in the 'MPON' pool, specifically in the 'MPON Lane', and the other in the 'MPOO' pool, specifically in the 'MPOO Lane'.
