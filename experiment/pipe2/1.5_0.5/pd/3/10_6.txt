The process begins with the "MSPN" sending a dismissal to the "MSPO". This is followed by the "MSPO" reviewing the dismissal. After the review, the "MSPO" makes a decision, which can result in two potential outcomes. 

The first outcome is the "MSPO" rejecting the dismissal of the "MSPN". The second outcome is the "MSPO" confirming the dismissal of the "MSPN". Regardless of the decision made by the "MSPO", the process will end after the decision is made. 

The tasks are divided between two pools, "MSPN" and "MSPO". The "MSPN" pool includes the task of sending a dismissal to the "MSPO". The "MSPO" pool encompasses the tasks of reviewing the dismissal, deciding on the dismissal, and either rejecting or confirming the dismissal of the "MSPN". 

The flow of the process is guided by sequence flows that dictate the order of tasks and decisions. There is also a message flow that occurs between the "MSPN" sending a dismissal and the "MSPO" reviewing the dismissal.
