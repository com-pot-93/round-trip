The process begins with "INQ transmits the transaction data request". After this, "IP checks the request". Depending on the results of this check, represented by "Check request result", there may be different outcomes. If the check is successful, the process moves to "IP answers the question". The process ends after this step. The tasks and events are organized into two separate pools, one for "INQ" and one for "IP". Within the "INQ" pool, the tasks and events are further organized into "INQ Lane". Similarly, within the "IP" pool, the tasks and events are organized into "IP Lane". The flow of the process is represented by sequence flows and message flows. The sequence flows show the order of the tasks and events, while the message flows show the interaction between different tasks.
