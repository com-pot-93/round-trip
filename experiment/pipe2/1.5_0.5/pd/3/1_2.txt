The process starts with the action of checking the defect and calculating the repair cost. After this, a decision is required based on the repair costs. This decision is made by the customer. Depending on the customer's decision, the next steps involve either repairing the hardware or configuring the software. After either of these actions, a system functionality test is performed. The result of this test determines the next action. If the test is successful, the process ends. If not, the process may need to loop back for further hardware repair or software configuration. The process involves two main actors, the customer and the CRS. The customer is responsible for deciding on the repair costs, while the CRS performs the rest of the tasks.
