The process begins at "The Evanstonian" with the "Room Service" lane. The first action is "Take down the order". Once the order is taken, it is then submitted to the kitchen with "Submit order ticket to the kitchen". Following this, the order is given to the sommelier with "Order to the sommelier". After the sommelier has the order, it is assigned to the waiter with "Assign the order to the waiter". The waiter then readies a cart with "Ready a cart". Once the cart is ready, it is delivered to the guest's room with "Deliver to the guest's room". After the delivery, the guest's account is debited with "Debit the guest's account". This marks the end of the process.
