The process takes place at The Evanstonian Hotel, specifically within the Room Service lane. It begins with the action of "Take Order". Once the order is taken, it is then submitted to the kitchen. After submission, the order is given to the Sommelier. The Sommelier's job is then to assign the order to a waiter. The waiter readies the cart and proceeds to deliver the order. Once the order is delivered, the final step is to debit the guest's account. This marks the end of the process.
