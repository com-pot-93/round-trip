The process begins with MPON sending the dismissal to MPOO. Once this action is completed, MPOO reviews the dismissal. Following the review, MPOO is faced with a decision. They can either oppose the dismissal of MPON or confirm the dismissal of MPON. If MPOO opposes the dismissal, the process ends. However, if MPOO confirms the dismissal, the process also ends. Therefore, the process concludes with either the opposition or confirmation of the dismissal by MPOO.
