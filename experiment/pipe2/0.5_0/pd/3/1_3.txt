The process begins at "start" in the "Room Service" lane of "The Evanstonian" pool. The first step is "Take room service order". After the order is taken, it is then "Submitted to the kitchen". The kitchen then "Gives the order to the sommelier". Once the sommelier has the order, they "Prepare the cart". After the cart is prepared, the order is then "Delivered". Once the order is delivered, the "Guest's account is debited". The process ends at "end".
