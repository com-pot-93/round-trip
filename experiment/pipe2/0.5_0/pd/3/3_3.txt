The process begins with "Claim registration". After this, the process moves to the "Examination by claims officer". Following the examination, the next step is "Writing settlement recommendation". Once the recommendation is written, it is then "Checking by senior claims officer". After the checking, the process reaches a decision point "Claim OK or Not OK". If the claim is not OK, the process loops back to the "Examination by claims officer". If the claim is OK, the process moves to the "Claim handling process". The process ends after the claim handling process.
