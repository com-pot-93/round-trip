The process begins with the "Take Order" step. After this, it moves to the "Order Includes Alcohol?" decision point. If the order includes alcohol, the "Order Wine" step is completed before moving to "Submit Order to Kitchen". If the order does not include alcohol, it directly moves to "Submit Order to Kitchen". After the order is submitted to the kitchen, the process continues with "Prepare Cart". Once the cart is prepared, the order is delivered during the "Deliver Order" step. The last step of the process is "Debit Guest Account". The process ends after this step. This entire process is managed by the "Room Service" with tasks divided between the "Room Service Manager" and the "Waiter".
