The process begins with "MPON sends the dismissal to the MPOO". Following this, "MPOO reviews the dismissal". After the review, a decision is made at the "Decision to oppose or confirm" stage. If the dismissal is opposed, "MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON" is carried out. Alternatively, if the dismissal is confirmed, "MPOO confirms the dismissal of MPON" is performed. Both these paths lead to the end of the process. Throughout the process, there's a message flow from the task of "MPON sends the dismissal to the MPOO" to "MPOO reviews the dismissal". This process involves two pools, namely "MPON" and "MPOO". The "MPOO" pool contains a lane titled "Dismissal review" which includes the tasks of dismissal review, decision making, opposing the dismissal, and confirming the dismissal.
