The process begins with the "MSPN sends a dismissal to MSPO". Once this action is completed, the "MSPO reviews the dismissal". After reviewing, the MSPO makes a decision, which is either to reject or confirm the dismissal. If the MSPO decides to reject the dismissal, the process moves to "MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN" and then it ends. If the MSPO decides to confirm the dismissal, the process moves to "MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN" and then it ends.
