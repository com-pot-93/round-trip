The process begins at "The Evanstonian" in the "Room Service" lane with "Take down the order". After this, the order ticket is submitted to the kitchen. Once the kitchen has the order, it is given to the sommelier. The sommelier then assigns the order to a waiter. The waiter readies a cart and then delivers the order. After the order has been delivered, the guest's account is debited. This marks the end of the process.
