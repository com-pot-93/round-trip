The process begins with the "Submit Vacation Request" action. Once this action is completed, the process moves to the "Review Vacation Request" action. After the vacation request has been reviewed, a decision is made at the "Request Decision" point. Depending on the decision made, the process either moves to the "Approve or Reject Request" action or the "Review Rejection Reasons" action. If the request is approved, the process ends. If the request is rejected, the process moves to the "Complete Management Procedures" action before ending. 

The process involves three different roles: Employee, Supervisor, and HR. The Employee is responsible for submitting the vacation request and reviewing any rejection reasons. The Supervisor is responsible for reviewing the vacation request and either approving or rejecting it. If the request is rejected, the HR is responsible for completing the management procedures.
