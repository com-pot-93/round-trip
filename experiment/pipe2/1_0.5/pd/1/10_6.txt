The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. After the dismissal has been sent, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, the MSPO makes a decision. If the MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN, the process ends. However, if the MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN, the process also ends. The actions of the MSPN are contained within the MSPN actions lane, while the actions of the MSPO are contained within the MSPO actions lane. The decision made by the MSPO is represented by the MSPO decision gateway. The process flow is represented by the sequence flows from start to end, with message flows indicating communication between tasks.
