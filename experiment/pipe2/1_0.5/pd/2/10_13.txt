The process begins with "INQ transmits the transaction data request". After this, "IP checks the request". Depending on the outcome of the "Check request outcome", "IP answers the question". The process ends after this step. The process involves two main entities, INQ and IP. INQ is responsible for transmitting the transaction data request and answering the question, while IP is responsible for checking the request. The flow of information between these entities is managed through a series of sequence flows and message flows.
