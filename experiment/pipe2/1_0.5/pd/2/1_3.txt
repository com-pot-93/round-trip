The process begins at "start" in "The Evanstonian" pool, specifically in the "Room Service" lane. The first action is "Take down the order". Once this is completed, the next step is to "Submit order ticket to the kitchen". After the order ticket has been submitted to the kitchen, the order is then given to the sommelier. The sommelier's task is followed by "Assign order to the waiter". Once the order has been assigned to the waiter, the next step is to "Ready the cart". After the cart has been readied, the order is then delivered to the guest. The final step in the process is to "Debit the guest's account". The process ends at "end".
