The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. After the dismissal has been sent, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, the MSPO makes a decision. If the MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN, the process ends. However, if the MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN, the process also ends. The decision of the MSPO, whether to reject or confirm the dismissal, is exclusive, meaning only one of these actions can be taken. The process flows from the MSPN to the MSPO, with the dismissal being the message that is transferred.
