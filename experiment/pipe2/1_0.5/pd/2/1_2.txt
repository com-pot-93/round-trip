The process begins with the "Check defect and calculate repair cost" activity. Once this is completed, the "Customer decision on repair cost" is made. Depending on the decision, either the "Hardware check and repair" or the "Software check and configuration" activity is performed. Both of these activities lead to the "System functionality test". The result of this test leads to the "System functionality test result" decision. Depending on the result, the process either ends, or returns to the "Hardware check and repair" or the "Software check and configuration" activity. If the process does not return to these activities, it ends. 

The process involves two main entities: the Customer and CRS. The Customer is involved in the "Check defect and calculate repair cost" activity and the "Customer decision on repair cost" decision. The CRS is responsible for the "Hardware check and repair", "Software check and configuration", and "System functionality test" activities, as well as the "System functionality test result" decision.
