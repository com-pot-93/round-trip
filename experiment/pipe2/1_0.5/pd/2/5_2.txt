The process begins with the "Submit Vacation Request" action. After this, the "Approve or Reject Request" action takes place. Depending on the decision made in the "Request Decision", the process may either proceed to "Review Rejection Reasons" or "Complete Management Procedures". If the "Review Rejection Reasons" action is taken, the process ends. Similarly, if the "Complete Management Procedures" action is taken, the process also ends.

The process involves three different roles: Employee, Supervisor, and HR. The Employee is responsible for "Submit Vacation Request" and "Review Rejection Reasons" actions. The Supervisor is responsible for the "Approve or Reject Request" action. The HR is responsible for the "Complete Management Procedures" action.
