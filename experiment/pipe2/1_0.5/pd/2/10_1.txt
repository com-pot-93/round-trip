The process begins with "MPON sends the dismissal to the MPOO". After this, "MPOO reviews the dismissal". Following the review, a decision is made at the "Decision to oppose or confirm dismissal" stage. If the decision is to oppose, then "MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON" and the process ends. If the decision is to confirm, then "MPOO confirms the dismissal of the MPON" and the process also ends. The process involves two main entities: MPON, who initiates the process, and MPOO, who reviews and makes the final decision.
