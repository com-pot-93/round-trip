The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. After receiving the dismissal, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, the MSPO has to make a decision. The decision could either be to reject the dismissal of the MSPN or to confirm it. If the MSPO rejects the dismissal, the process ends. However, if the MSPO confirms the dismissal, the process also comes to an end.
