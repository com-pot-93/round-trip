The process begins with the "Submit Vacation Request" action. Once this action is completed, the process moves to the "Review Request" action. After the request has been reviewed, a decision is made at the "Request Decision" point. Depending on the decision, the process either moves to the "Approve/Reject Request" action or the "Review Rejection Reasons" action. If the request is approved, the process ends. If the request is rejected, the process moves to the "Complete Management Procedures" action before ending. 

The process involves three different roles: Employee, Supervisor, and HR. The Employee is responsible for submitting the vacation request and reviewing any rejection reasons. The Supervisor is responsible for reviewing the request and making the decision to approve or reject it. HR is responsible for completing management procedures if the request is rejected.
