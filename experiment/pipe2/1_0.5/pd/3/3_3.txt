The process begins with the "Claim Registration". After the claim is registered, it is then examined in the "Examine Claim" step. Following the examination, a "Write Settlement Recommendation" is made. This recommendation is then checked in the "Check Recommendation" step. 

After the recommendation has been checked, the process reaches a decision point, "Claim OK or Not OK". If the claim is not OK, the process loops back to the "Examine Claim" step. If the claim is OK, the process moves forward to the "Handle Claim" step. 

Finally, after the claim has been handled, the process ends.
