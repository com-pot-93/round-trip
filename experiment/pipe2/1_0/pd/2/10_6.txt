The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. After the dismissal has been sent, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, the MSPO has to make a decision. This decision could either be to reject the dismissal of the MSPN or to confirm it. If the MSPO rejects the dismissal, the process ends. However, if the MSPO confirms the dismissal, the process also ends. The process flow is divided into two lanes, one for MSPN and the other for MSPO. The MSPN lane includes the start of the process and the task of sending the dismissal. The MSPO lane includes the tasks of reviewing, rejecting or confirming the dismissal, the decision-making point, and the end of the process.
