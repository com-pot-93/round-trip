The process begins at "The Evanstonian" in the "Room Service" lane with "Take down the order". After this, the order ticket is submitted to the kitchen. Once the kitchen has the order, it is given to the sommelier. The sommelier then assigns the order to the waiter. The waiter is responsible for readying the cart. Once the cart is ready, the order is delivered to the guest. The final step in the process is to debit the guest's account. The process ends after the guest's account has been debited.
