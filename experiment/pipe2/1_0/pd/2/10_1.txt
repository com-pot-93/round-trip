The process begins with "MPON sends the dismissal". After this, "MPOO reviews the dismissal". Following the review, a decision is made to either oppose or confirm the dismissal. If the decision is to oppose, "MPOO opposes the dismissal" is carried out and the process ends. If the decision is to confirm, "MPOO confirms the dismissal" is carried out and the process ends. The process involves two main entities, MPON and MPOO. MPON initiates the process and MPOO carries out the review, makes the decision, and carries out the subsequent action based on the decision.
