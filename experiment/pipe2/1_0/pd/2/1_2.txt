The process begins with the "Check defect and calculate repair cost" activity. After this, a decision is made at the "Customer decision" point. Depending on the decision, the process can either move to "Hardware repair" or "Software configuration". Both these activities lead to the "System functionality test". After the test, another decision is made at the "Error detection" point. If there are no errors, the process ends. However, if errors are detected, the process can loop back to either "Hardware repair" or "Software configuration". This process is part of the "CRS" pool, specifically the "Repair process" lane.
