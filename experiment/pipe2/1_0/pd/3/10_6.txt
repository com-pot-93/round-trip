The process begins with the action "MSPN sends a dismissal to the MSPO". After this, the "MSPO reviews the dismissal". Following the review, a decision is made by the MSPO. This decision can lead to two possible outcomes. In one scenario, the "MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN", and in the other scenario, the "MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN". Both scenarios lead to the end of the process.
