The process begins with the "Submit Vacation Request" action. After this, the "Review Request" action takes place. Following the review, a decision is made at the "Request Decision" point. Depending on the decision, the "Approve/Reject Request" action is carried out. If the request is rejected, the "Review Rejection Reasons" action is performed. After all these steps, the "Complete Management Procedures" action is carried out, marking the end of the process.

The process involves three roles: Employee, Supervisor, and HR. The Employee is responsible for "Submit Vacation Request" and "Review Rejection Reasons" actions. The Supervisor is responsible for "Review Request" and "Approve/Reject Request" actions. The HR is responsible for the "Complete Management Procedures" action.
