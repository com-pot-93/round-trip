The process begins with MPON sending the dismissal to the MPOO. After this, the MPOO reviews the dismissal. Following the review, a decision is made by the MPOO. This decision can result in two outcomes. In one scenario, the MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON, and in the other scenario, the MPOO confirms the dismissal of MPON. Regardless of the outcome, the process ends after the MPOO's decision.
