The process begins with the action of checking the defect and calculating the repair cost. Once this is done, the customer makes a decision on the repair cost. This decision leads to a critical point where two possible actions can occur. The first action is to check and repair the hardware, and the second action is to check and configure the software. Both these actions eventually lead to testing the system functionality. After the system functionality is tested, another critical decision point is reached. This decision point can lead to three possible outcomes. The first outcome is the end of the process. The second outcome is to go back and check and repair the hardware. The third outcome is to go back and check and configure the software.
