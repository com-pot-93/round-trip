The process begins with "MPON sends the dismissal". After this, "MPOO reviews the dismissal". Following the review, a decision is made to either oppose or confirm the dismissal. If the decision is to oppose, "MPOO opposes the dismissal" is carried out and the process ends. If the decision is to confirm, "MPOO confirms the dismissal" is carried out and the process also ends. The actions of MPON and MPOO are carried out in their respective lanes. The dismissal message is sent from MPON to MPOO for review.
