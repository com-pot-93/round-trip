The process begins with the "Submit Vacation Request" action. Once this action is completed, it moves on to the "Review Request" action. After the request has been reviewed, a decision is made at the "Request Decision" point. Depending on the decision, the process either moves to the "Approve/Reject Request" action or the "Review Rejection Reasons" action. If the request is approved, the process ends. If the request is rejected, the "Review Rejection Reasons" action is performed. After reviewing the rejection reasons, the "Complete Management Procedures" action is performed, and the process ends. 

The process involves three roles: Employee, Supervisor, and HR. The Employee is responsible for submitting the vacation request and reviewing the rejection reasons if the request is rejected. The Supervisor is responsible for reviewing the request and making the decision to approve or reject it. If the request is rejected, the HR is responsible for completing the management procedures.
