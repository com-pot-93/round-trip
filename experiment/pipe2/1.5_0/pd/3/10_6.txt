The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. After receiving the dismissal, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, the MSPO makes a decision. This decision could either be to reject the dismissal of the MSPN or to confirm the dismissal of the MSPN. The process ends after the MSPO has made their decision. The MSPN and MSPO activities are carried out in their respective lanes.
