The process begins with the MSPN sending a dismissal to the MSPO. After receiving the dismissal, the MSPO reviews it. Following the review, the MSPO makes a decision. If the MSPO rejects the dismissal of the MSPN, the process ends. However, if the MSPO confirms the dismissal of the MSPN, the process also ends. The decision of the MSPO, whether to reject or confirm the dismissal, is exclusive and only one path can be followed.
