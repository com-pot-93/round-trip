The process begins with the "Submit Vacation Request" action. After this, the "Approve or Reject Request" action takes place. Depending on the decision made in the "Request Decision", the process can go in two directions. If the request is rejected, the "Review Rejection Reasons" action is performed. If the request is approved, the "Complete Management Procedures" action is performed. Both these actions lead to the end of the process. 

The process involves three roles: Employee, Supervisor, and HR. The Employee is responsible for "Submit Vacation Request" and "Review Rejection Reasons" actions. The Supervisor is responsible for the "Approve or Reject Request" action. The HR is responsible for the "Complete Management Procedures" action.
