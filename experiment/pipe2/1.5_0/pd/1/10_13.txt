The process begins with "INQ" transmitting the transaction data request. This action is carried out in the "INQ Lane" of the "INQ" pool. Once this is done, the request is checked by "IP" in the "IP Lane" of the "IP" pool. After the request has been checked, the outcome is determined at the "Check request outcome" point. Depending on the outcome, "IP" then answers the question. This response is also carried out in the "IP Lane". The process concludes with the "end" event. Throughout this process, there are message flows between the transmission of the transaction data request and the checking of the request, as well as between the answering of the question and the transmission of the transaction data request.
