The process begins with the action "MPON sends the dismissal to the MPOO". After this, the action "MPOO reviews the dismissal" takes place. Following the review, a decision is made by the MPOO. Depending on the decision, one of two actions can occur. If the MPOO opposes the dismissal of the MPON, the action "MPOO opposes the dismissal of MPON" takes place. Alternatively, if the MPOO confirms the dismissal of the MPON, the action "MPOO confirms the dismissal of the MPON" occurs. Both of these actions lead to the end of the process.
