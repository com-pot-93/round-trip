The process begins at "The Evanstonian" in the "Room Service" lane with "Take down the order". After this, the next step is to "Submit order ticket to the kitchen". Once the order ticket has been submitted, the process continues with "Give order to the sommelier". After the sommelier has received the order, the next step is to "Assign order to the waiter". The waiter then proceeds to "Prepare the cart". Once the cart is prepared, the waiter will "Deliver order to the guest". The final step in the process is to "Debit the guest's account". After the guest's account has been debited, the process ends.
