The process begins with "MSPN sends dismissal". After this, "MSPO reviews the dismissal". Depending on the conditions, it can take either of two paths. In one path, "MSPO rejects the dismissal", after which a specific process is triggered. Simultaneously, in a different path, "MSPO confirms the dismissal", followed by a separate set of actions. Both paths eventually converge this process to a point where it ends.
