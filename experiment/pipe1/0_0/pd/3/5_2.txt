The process begins with the "start" event. The first action taken is "employee submits vacation request". After this, the process moves to "register the requirement". Once the requirement is registered, the "supervisor receives the request". The supervisor then proceeds to "approve the request". 

At this point, the process reaches a decision point. One path leads to "return application", while the other leads to "generate notification". If the "return application" path is taken, the process moves to another decision point. From here, the process either ends or moves to "HR completes the respective management procedures". 

If the "generate notification" path is taken from the first decision point, the process directly moves to "HR completes the respective management procedures" and then reaches the end. 

In summary, this process describes the steps taken from an employee submitting a vacation request to the HR completing the respective management procedures, with decision points for returning the application or generating a notification.
