The process begins with the guest making a call. Following this, the manager takes down the order. The manager then submits an order ticket to the kitchen and gives an order to the sommelier. After this, the manager assigns the order to the waiter. 

At this point, the kitchen and the sommelier are doing their tasks in parallel. Once they have completed their tasks, the waiter readies the cart. The waiter then delivers the cart and returns to the room service station. 

After returning to the room service station, the waiter may wait to do the billing or directly debit the guest account. The process ends once the guest account has been debited.
