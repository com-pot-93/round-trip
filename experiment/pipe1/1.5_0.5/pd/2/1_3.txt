The process begins with a guest making a call. Following this, the manager takes down the order. The manager then submits an order ticket to the kitchen and also gives an order to the sommelier. After this, the manager assigns the order to the waiter.

In parallel, the kitchen and the sommelier start doing their tasks. The waiter also begins readying the cart. Once both these tasks are completed, the waiter delivers the cart. After delivering the cart, the waiter returns to the room service station.

At this point, the waiter may wait to do the billing or directly debit the guest account. The process concludes once the guest account has been debited.
