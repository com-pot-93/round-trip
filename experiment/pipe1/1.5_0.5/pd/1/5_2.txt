The process begins with the "start" event. The first action taken is the "employee submits vacation request". After this, the next step is to "register the requirement". Following this, the "supervisor receives the request". The supervisor then "approves the request". 

At this point, the process reaches a decision point. One path leads to the "return application" action and then to another decision point. From here, the process can either end or proceed to "HR completes the respective management procedures" before ending. 

The other path from the initial decision point leads to "generate notification". This then leads to "HR completes the respective management procedures" and then the process ends.
