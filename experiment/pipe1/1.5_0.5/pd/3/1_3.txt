The process begins with a guest making a call. This call is received by the manager who then takes down the order. After noting down the order, the manager then submits an order ticket to the kitchen and also gives an order to the sommelier. Subsequently, the manager assigns the order to the waiter.

Following this, the kitchen and the sommelier start doing their tasks in parallel. Once they finish, the waiter readies the cart. The process can then branch out in two directions. In one, the waiter delivers the cart and then returns to the room service station. After returning, the waiter may wait to do the billing or directly debit the guest account, marking the end of the process. In the other direction, the waiter directly debits the guest account after readying the cart, which also marks the end of the process.
