The process begins with the "start" event. The first action taken is by the claims officer who registers the claim. After the claim is registered, the claims officer then examines the claim. Once the examination is complete, the process reaches a decision point.

Depending on the outcome of this decision, the process can go in two directions. One path leads to the claims officer writing a settlement recommendation. After the recommendation is written, the senior claims officer checks the recommendation. Following this, the senior claims officer marks the claim. This leads to another decision point.

If the claim is marked appropriately, the claim handling process proceeds and the process ends with the "end" event. However, if there are issues with the claim, the senior claims officer sends the recommendation back. This loops the process back to the first decision point where the claims officer writes the settlement recommendation. The process then repeats until the claim is marked appropriately and the claim handling process can proceed to its conclusion.
