The process begins with the event "start". Following this, the task "MSPN sends dismissal" is carried out. After this, the task "MSPO reviews the dismissal" is performed. 

The process then reaches a decision point. Depending on the outcome, one of two paths is followed. 

In the first path, the task "MSPO rejects the dismissal" is performed. After this, the process reaches another decision point. 

In the second path, the task "MSPO confirms the dismissal" is carried out. This also leads to the second decision point. 

Regardless of the path taken, the process concludes with the event "end".
