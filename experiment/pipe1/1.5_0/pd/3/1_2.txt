The process begins with the customer bringing a defective computer. The CRS then checks the defect and hands out a repair cost calculation. From here, the process can take two paths. 

In the first path, the customer takes the computer home. In the second path, the process splits into two parallel paths. In one, the hardware is checked and then repaired. In the other, the software is checked and then configured. 

After both paths are completed, the system functionality is tested. If the test is successful, the repair is finished. If not, the process returns to the point where the repair cost calculation is handed out. 

Finally, the process ends when the repair is finished.
