The process begins with the "start" event. The first task is for the employee to submit a vacation request. Once this is done, the requirement is registered. The supervisor then receives the request and approves it. After the approval, there are two possible paths. 

In the first path, the application is returned. After this, the process moves to the next stage where HR completes the respective management procedures. 

In the second path, a notification is generated. This is followed by HR completing the respective management procedures. 

Both paths converge at the same point and the process ends with the "end" event.
