The process begins with a guest making a call. The manager then takes down the order. After this, the manager submits an order ticket to the kitchen and gives an order to the sommelier. The manager then assigns the order to the waiter. 

At this point, the kitchen and the sommelier start doing their tasks. This happens in parallel with the waiter readying the cart. Once both these tasks are completed, the waiter delivers the cart. 

After delivering the cart, the waiter returns to the room service station. Here, the waiter may wait to do the billing or directly debit the guest account. The process ends after the guest's account is debited.
