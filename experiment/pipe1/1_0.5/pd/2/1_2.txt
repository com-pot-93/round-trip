The process begins with the customer bringing a defective computer. The CRS then checks the defect. Following this, the CRS hands out a repair cost calculation. From here, the process can go in two directions. 

In one direction, the customer takes the computer home. In the other direction, the process moves to checking the hardware and repairing it. Simultaneously, the software is checked and configured. 

After both the hardware and software have been addressed, the system functionality is tested. If the repair is finished, the process moves towards the end. If not, it loops back to the point where the hardware and software are checked and configured. 

Finally, once the repair is finished, the process ends.
