The process begins with the employee submitting a vacation request. This request is then registered. The supervisor receives the request and approves it. After the approval, there are two possible paths. 

In the first path, the application is returned. After the return, the process moves to the next step where HR completes the respective management procedures. 

In the second path, a notification is generated. After the notification, the process also moves to the step where HR completes the respective management procedures. 

Finally, the process ends.
