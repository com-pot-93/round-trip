The process begins with the 'start' event. The first task in the process is for the employee to submit a vacation request. Once the employee submits the request, the next task is to register the requirement. After the requirement is registered, the supervisor receives the request. 
Upon receiving the request, the supervisor then approves the request. After the supervisor approves the request, the process reaches an exclusive gateway. From this gateway, the process can either proceed to the task of returning the application or generating a notification. 
If the process proceeds to return the application, it then reaches another exclusive gateway before ending. If the process proceeds to generate a notification, the next task is for HR to complete the respective management procedures. After HR completes these procedures, the process reaches the second exclusive gateway before ending. 
The process ends with the 'end' event. Throughout this process, there are no pools or message flows involved.
