The process begins with the start event, followed by the task where MPON sends the dismissal. After this, the process moves on to the next task where MPOO reviews the dismissal. Then, the process reaches a decision point. Depending on the outcome, the process either moves to the task where MPOO opposes the dismissal or to the task where MPOO confirms the dismissal. After either of these tasks, the process reaches another decision point. Depending on the outcome of this decision, the process either continues from the task where MPOO opposes the dismissal or from the task where MPOO confirms the dismissal. Finally, the process ends with the end event.
