The process starts with 'MPON sends the dismissal' which is then forwarded to 'MPOO reviews the dismissal'. Depending on the review, the process can take one of two paths. Either 'MPOO opposes the dismissal' or 'MPOO confirms the dismissal'. The first case leads to another decision point after 'MPOO opposes the dismissal'. Meanwhile, in the case of confirmation, the process leads directly to the decision point. In both cases, the final stage is the 'end' event.
