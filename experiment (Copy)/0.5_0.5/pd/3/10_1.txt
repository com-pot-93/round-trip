The process begins with MPON sending the dismissal. After this step, MPOO reviews the dismissal. There is a decision to be made where two separate paths can be taken. 

In the first scenario, MPOO opposes the dismissal. Following the opposition, another decision is made.

Alternatively on the second path, MPOO can choose to confirm the dismissal. After this step, in both paths, a decision point is reached.

Lastly, regardless of the taken path in previous decision point, the process ends.
