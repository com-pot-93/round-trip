The process begins with the event "start". Following this, the task "MPON sends the dismissal" is performed. After this, the task "MPOO reviews the dismissal" is carried out. 

The process then reaches a decision point. Depending on the outcome, one of two paths is followed. 

In the first path, the task "MPOO opposes the dismissal" is performed. After this, the process reaches another decision point. 

In the second path, the task "MPOO confirms the dismissal" is performed. This also leads to the second decision point. 

Regardless of the path taken, the process ends with the event "end".
