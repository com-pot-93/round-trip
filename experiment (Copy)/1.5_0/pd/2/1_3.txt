The process begins with a guest making a call. The manager then takes down the order. After this, the manager submits an order ticket to the kitchen and gives an order to the sommelier. The manager then assigns the order to the waiter. 

At this point, the kitchen and the sommelier are doing their tasks, and the waiter readies the cart. These tasks can be done in parallel. Once both tasks are completed, the waiter delivers the cart. After delivering the cart, the waiter returns to the room service station. 

Here, the waiter may wait to do the billing or directly debit the guest account. Both these tasks are exclusive, meaning only one of them will be performed. The process ends after the waiter debits the guest account.
