The process begins with a guest making a call. Following this, the manager takes down the order. The manager then submits an order ticket to the kitchen and gives an order to the sommelier. After this, the manager assigns the order to the waiter. 

At this point, the kitchen and the sommelier start doing their tasks. This happens in parallel with the waiter readying the cart. Once both these tasks are completed, the waiter delivers the cart. 

After delivering the cart, the waiter returns to the room service station. Here, there are two possible paths. The waiter may wait to do the billing or directly debit the guest account. Regardless of the path taken, the process ends after the guest's account is debited.
