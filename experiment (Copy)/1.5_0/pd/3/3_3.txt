The process begins with the "start" event. The first action taken is by the claims officer who registers the claim. After the claim is registered, the claims officer then examines the claim. Following the examination, there are two possible paths.

In the first path, the claims officer writes a settlement recommendation. This recommendation is then checked by the senior claims officer. After checking the recommendation, the senior claims officer marks the claim. Following this, the claim handling process proceeds and finally reaches the "end" event.

In the second path, after the claim is examined, the senior claims officer sends the recommendation back. This leads back to the point where the claims officer writes a settlement recommendation, and the process continues as described in the first path.
