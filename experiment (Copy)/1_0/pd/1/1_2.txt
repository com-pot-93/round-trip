The process begins with the customer bringing a defective computer. The CRS then checks the defect. Following this, the CRS hands out a repair cost calculation. The customer then takes the computer home. 

Meanwhile, the process splits into two paths. In the first path, the hardware is checked and then repaired. In the second path, the software is checked and then configured. 

After both paths are completed, the system functionality is tested. If the test is successful, the repair is declared finished. If not, the process loops back to the point where the hardware and software are checked and configured. 

Finally, once the repair is finished, the process ends.
