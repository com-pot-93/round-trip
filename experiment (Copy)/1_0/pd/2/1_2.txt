The process begins with the customer bringing a defective computer. The CRS then checks the defect. Following this, the CRS hands out a repair cost calculation. Depending on the outcome, the customer may take the computer home or the process may proceed to further steps. 

If the process continues, there are two parallel paths. In the first path, the hardware is checked and then repaired. In the second path, the software is checked and then configured. 

After both paths are completed, the system functionality is tested. Depending on the outcome of the test, the process may loop back to the hardware and software checks or it may proceed to the final step. 

The final step is the completion of the repair. After this, the process ends.
